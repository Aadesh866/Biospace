## 🎓 LESSONS LEARNED

1. **Content Structure**: Consistent structure across all services makes maintenance easier
2. **Image Reuse**: Local images can be reused across services intelligently
3. **FAQ Value**: Service-specific FAQs add huge value (actual customer questions)

## 💡 KEY DECISIONS MADE

3. **Accordion FAQs**: Save space, better mobile experience
4. **Consistent Layout**: All 6 services follow same structure
5. **Local Images Only**: No Pexels, all actual project photos
