### FAQs (6 questions)
1. **How much does false ceiling cost per sq ft?**
   False ceiling costs range from ₹150 to ₹300 per sq ft depending on design complexity and lighting. Basic gypsum ceiling costs ₹150-180, while designer POP ceilings with multiple levels cost ₹250-300 per sq ft.

2. **What is the difference between gypsum and POP?**
   Gypsum is prefabricated boards that are quick to install and more durable, while POP is wet plaster that allows intricate designs but takes longer to dry. We recommend gypsum for larger areas and POP for decorative elements.

3. **Can you install AC ducts in false ceiling?**
   Yes, we plan false ceiling height to accommodate AC ducts, plumbing pipes, and electrical conduits. We leave access panels for future maintenance of these services.

4. **How long does installation take?**
   A standard 12x12 ft room takes 2-3 days including framework, boarding, finishing, and painting. Multiple rooms can be done simultaneously to reduce overall timeline.

5. **Do you provide warranty on false ceiling?**
   Yes, we provide a 10-year structural warranty covering the framework and gypsum boards against sagging, cracks, or structural failure. Paint warranty is 1 year against peeling or discoloration.

6. **Can LED lights be added later to existing ceiling?**
   Yes, LED strips can be retrofitted into existing false ceilings if there is cove space. However, we recommend planning lighting during installation for best results and to hide all wiring properly.
