## 📐 DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Typography
- **Headlines**: font-heading (Montserrat), bold, 2.5-4rem
- **Subheadings**: font-heading, semibold, 1.5-2rem
- **Body**: Inter, regular, 1rem-1.125rem
- **Captions**: Inter, medium, 0.875rem

### Colors
- **Primary**: brand-orange (#FF6B35)
- **Secondary**: brand-gray-900 (#111827)
- **Background**: white, brand-gray-50
- **Accents**: brand-orange-500, brand-orange-600
- **Text**: brand-gray-900 (headings), brand-gray-600 (body)

### Spacing
- **Section padding**: py-16 md:py-24 (top/bottom)
- **Container max-width**: max-w-7xl
- **Grid gaps**: gap-8 md:gap-12
- **Card padding**: p-6 md:p-8

### Animations
- **Fade in on scroll**: Framer Motion `whileInView`
- **Hover effects**: scale(1.05), translateY(-5px)
- **Transitions**: duration 300-500ms
- **Stagger children**: delay 100ms per item
